## Why it works

A message heard once is a claim. The same message heard on LinkedIn at work, on YouTube in the evening and on Instagram at the weekend starts to feel like common knowledge. Repetition across contexts makes a message feel true and a brand feel familiar.

Being everywhere also sends a signal. Buyers assume a company that shows up across every channel is established and well funded, which makes it feel like a safe choice. Safety matters a lot in B2B, where picking the wrong vendor can cost someone their reputation.

Finally, B2B purchases are made by committees. Reaching only one or two people on one channel leaves the rest of the committee cold. Syncing one audience everywhere warms them all.

## Steps

1. **Start from your scored account list.** Use tiers A to C, not your whole market.
2. **Define the buying committee titles** for each tier, for example CEO, CFO and VP Finance.
3. **Match those people to their identities on each platform** using a tool built for it (Primer, Clay Ads or influ2). This is what makes "the same people everywhere" possible.
4. **Give each channel a job.** For example: LinkedIn for thought leadership, YouTube for brand video, Meta for off-desk reach and lead generation, Google for search, Reddit for native-feeling posts.
5. **Keep targeting tight outside LinkedIn.** On Meta, use only your account lists and website retargeting. Nothing else.
6. **Respect audience minimums.** Platforms other than LinkedIn need a minimum audience size before they serve, so group accounts by tier rather than running one-account campaigns.

## Example

Tabs builds target account audiences in Primer synced to their company tiers A, B and C, defines buying committee titles (CEO, CFO, VP Finance), then activates the same audience on LinkedIn, Meta, YouTube, Google and Reddit. On Meta, cost per lead came in at about a quarter of LinkedIn's once they switched from affinity audiences to CRM account lists.

Datadog applies the same idea at the level of individual people. Every week they push the contacts most likely to book a meeting into Vector, which targets those specific people with ads on Facebook, Instagram, Reddit and YouTube, personalised to their pain point, segment and role. On LinkedIn they run account-level ads to microsites, and they add direct mail aimed at booking meetings.

## Prerequisites

- An account scoring and tiering framework.
- Budget to run several channels at once.
- An audience-matching tool, since platforms do not share identities.

## How to measure

- Reach and frequency within target accounts, per channel.
- Website visits from target accounts over time.
- Account progression from one tier stage to the next.
- Watch for platform over-reporting: every platform claims more conversions than can be independently confirmed.

## Pitfalls

- Broadcasting on new channels without audience parity, so you reach random people instead of your accounts.
- Using platform-native interest or affinity targeting for a specific ICP.
- Comparing channels on platform-reported cost per lead. Cheap leads are not the same as cheap pipeline.
