## Why it works

More and more people let an AI assistant do the searching and the choosing. About 37% of consumers now start a search with an AI tool instead of Google, and nearly 50% of Gen Z. Most people do not then compare ten options. They try the one the assistant suggests, because it is good enough and comparing takes effort. Being that suggestion matters more than ranking first on a results page that people no longer click.

Assistants learn mostly from what is already on the public web, and recommend the brands that appear most often across the most credible sources. That is the same pattern that makes people trust what they hear often and from many voices: frequency builds familiarity, and many independent mentions read as proof.

Visitors who arrive from an assistant are also unusually ready to act. They come with a specific task and a recommendation already made.

## Steps

1. **Ship and announce often.** Every launch, post and mention adds to your footprint on the public web. None of it converts this week, but it shows up months later in the next model that ships, and then it compounds.
2. **Get mentioned on credible third-party sites,** not just your own. Recommendation tracks how often you appear across sources other people trust.
3. **Build a real presence on Reddit.** It is one of the sources AI models draw on most; Semrush reported it as the most cited domain last year. Join and start useful threads where your buyers ask for help.
4. **Treat it as good search engine optimisation.** Assistants lean on what they learned in training first and only search the web when that falls short, so the basics of being findable still apply.
5. **Track it loosely.** Ask an assistant the same question ten times and you get the same answer only about 73% of the time, so tools that count mentions are directional at best. Watch sign-ups from AI referrals as the real signal.

## Example

Browserbase made agent-led growth its one big bet. They "make noise": 26 launches in one quarter, up 64% on the quarter before. On Reddit they started from zero; one month and four threads in, they had 8,700 views, 46 upvotes and 117 comments, and three of the four threads ranked in the top three on Google for their search terms. Visitors from AI assistants sign up at 1.66%, against 0.15% from traditional search, roughly ten times the conversion.

## Prerequisites

- A product people would plausibly ask an assistant to find.
- Someone who can post and engage authentically in communities.

## How to measure

- Sign-ups and conversion rate from AI assistant referrals.
- Branded mentions across third-party sites and communities over time.
- Rough share of assistant answers that mention you for your key tasks.

## Pitfalls

- Expecting results this month. This compounds slowly.
- Spammy or promotional Reddit posts, which get removed and damage trust.
- Over-trusting tools that claim to measure AI visibility precisely.
