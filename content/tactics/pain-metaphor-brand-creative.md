## Why it works

Most B2B ads look the same: a screenshot, a headline about efficiency and a demo button. In a feed of near-identical ads, the one that looks different is the one people remember.

A concrete image does more work than an abstract claim. "Reduce inventory waste" is processed as words and quickly forgotten. "Your margins spoil like your food" creates a picture in the mind, so the idea is stored twice and recalled more easily.

Humour and surprise create a positive feeling. People then judge the brand partly by that feeling, long before they compare features.

## Steps

1. **Find the moment of pain.** Write down the specific, everyday moment your buyer feels the problem. For example, "that moment when you over-ordered again and have a full stock of spoiled food".
2. **Find a concrete image for it.** Something visual and familiar that captures the feeling or the fix.
3. **Write a headline that links the image to the business outcome.** For example, "Your margins get spoiled the same way as your food."
4. **Keep it simple enough to get in two seconds.** If it needs explaining, simplify.
5. **Adapt per channel.** Brand video on YouTube, vertical cut for Meta and Instagram, native-feeling version for Reddit.
6. **Test a few concepts with real buyers** before investing heavily. Check that the joke resonates with them, not just your team.

## Example

Tabs' "Right on the money" video shows a claw machine picking up colourful piggy banks. You get it right away: Tabs works with revenue and helps you collect it. For Apicbase, a food-service operating system, the pain of over-ordering became "That moment when you over-ordered again, and have a full stock of spoiled food" with the headline "Your margins get spoiled the same way as your food."

## Prerequisites

- Deep understanding of the buyer's day-to-day pain, ideally from sales calls.
- Willingness to invest in creative production.

## How to measure

- Ad recall or brand lift studies where available.
- Engagement and view-through rates versus your standard creative.
- Branded search and direct traffic from target accounts over time.

## Pitfalls

- "Our buyers are conservative." Before the 1950s, consumer ads were as dry as B2B ads are now. Creativity works when the joke resonates.
- A metaphor so clever nobody gets it.
- Humour that makes fun of the buyer rather than their problem.
