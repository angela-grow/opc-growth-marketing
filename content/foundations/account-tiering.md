Most tactics in this library assume you know which accounts to aim at and how warm they are. You cannot treat every account the same way: each has a different level of awareness of your product and is at a different stage of their buying journey.

## Three groups, three goals

| Group | Who is in it | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Cluster ICP** | Fits your ideal customer profile, but has little or no engagement, no relationship and no evidence of need. | Create awareness until they hit an engagement threshold. |
| **Future pipeline** | Fits your ICP and has hit the engagement threshold. | Engage the buying committee and validate the need. |
| **Active focus** | Fits, is engaged, has a relationship with a champion or decision-maker, and the need is known. | Create sales opportunities. |

Two questions move an account between groups: *Is the account aware of us?* and *Is the product need known?*

Marketing and sales should define the engagement threshold together: which behaviours an account must show, at account and contact level. It should never come down to a rep saying "I think this account is engaged."

## How to build a score

1. **Analyse your closed-won deals.** Find the attributes your best customers share. Tabs ran a regression analysis on closed-won customers, plus the same analysis on Gong calls from later-stage deals.
2. **Choose your scoring inputs.** For example: pricing page changes, funding round, company age, growth speed, firmographics (industry, size, location) and first-party engagement (website visits, ad engagement).
3. **Give every account a dynamic score and tier** in your CRM. Tabs uses tiers A to F: A to C is their serviceable market, and A to B are the strategic accounts they prioritise.
4. **Keep it to signals your team can act on.** Slicing accounts by more signals than you can operationalise adds noise, not focus.

## A note on intent data

Tabs' growth lead has used several third-party intent data providers and found they did not work for ABM. They rely on first-party data instead: website engagement plus engagement with their paid channels.
